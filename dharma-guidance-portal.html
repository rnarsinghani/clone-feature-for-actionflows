Our approach

Ancient wisdom, held with modern care.

The Bhagavad Gita is not a book of shortcuts. It is a conversation about how to act when life is complex: with presence instead of panic, compassion instead of cruelty, and courage instead of avoidance.

Dharma translates those enduring ideas into plain language while honoring the many ways people understand and live this tradition. This local beta selects from reviewed reflections; it does not diagnose, judge, or declare anyone's single “right” path.

Safety and scope

For adults in India, this reflection is not care, crisis, or professional advice.

⊕

For health questions

For symptoms, diagnosis, treatment, medication, diet, fasting, pregnancy, or mental-health concerns, it directs you to qualified human care rather than offering an interpretation.

◇

For urgent danger

In India, call 112 for immediate danger or medical emergencies. For mental-health support, Tele-MANAS: 14416 or 1800-89-14416. Do not wait for this page.
